# Cross-model rubric ratings

This directory contains paired five-dimension ratings for 459 cases per evaluated model, totaling 2,295 artifacts. `expert_1` and `expert_2` are model-based scoring channel identifiers.

The scoring prompt uses the requirement, target AADL context, generated AADL+AGREE artifact, and rubric. It does not explicitly supply generator identity, validator status, or repair history.

- `review_inputs.csv`: recorded inputs.
- `ratings.csv`: individual dimension scores, brief reasons, and historical computed flags.
- `summary.csv`: historical aggregates using non-strict end-to-end usability.
- `system_manifest.csv`: generator names and result sources.

The current manuscript uses strict semantic usability: mean weighted score at least 4 and every mean dimension at least 3. Strict end-to-end usability adds final validation. Recomputed results and agreement statistics are in `paper/data/statistics/`, generated by `python paper/scripts/recompute_statistics.py`.

Original ratings are preserved. Historical non-strict usability and floating-point disagreement flags are not substituted for recomputed manuscript definitions. The Qwen artifacts overlap the RQ1 Full set; they are not independent additional cases.
